# T-209 — UI colour themes

Status: in-progress · Phase: 2 · Depends: — · Area: shell · Size: M · Owner: builder

## Goal
The owner can choose a colour theme in Settings; the whole shell changes with it at runtime, and every theme keeps the accessibility rules (legible text, an unmistakable focus ring). Owner request, 2026-11-06. Today `Theme.qml` is one fixed dark "Dreamglass" palette (plus the high-contrast variant, if built under Accessibility).

## Read first
- `docs/specs/04-design-system.md` §3.1 colour tokens, §3.6 glass, §3.8 (new glass layers and shadows use tokens too), §4 focus (the ring is white and never colour alone), §7 accessibility (contrast, high-contrast theme), §8
- `shell/qml/Theme.qml` (every `readonly property color`), where hard-coded colours might still hide in QML (`grep` for `#` colours and `Qt.rgba` outside Theme), `DeviceSettings`, the category tints / `defaultTint`, `tst_theme*`, the token lint (`lint-tokens`)

## Scope
1. **Theme as data:** a `ThemeSet` (QML object or JSON resource) per theme holding every colour token that may change: `background`, `text`, `textDim`, glass fills and borders, `glassOpaque`, `panel`, `scrim`s, `defaultTint` and the blob colours, `primaryFill`/`onPrimary`, `dangerFill`, `warningText`, `errorText`. Not themeable: the focus ring colour (white, plus its width), sizes, type, motion, and the rule "focus is never colour alone". `Theme.qml` exposes the same property names as now, driven by the current set, so no screen changes.
2. **Built-in themes (start with five):** **Dreamglass** (today's, the default), **Midnight** (true black background for OLED, neutral glass), **Aurora** (deep teal and violet tints), **Ember** (warm dark red/orange tints), **Daylight** (a light theme: dark text on frosted white glass over a light backdrop; the biggest change: check every component, the ring needs a dark outline variant here and the rule becomes "ring colour with a 2 px opposite-lightness halo"). If Daylight cannot meet the rules in this task, ship four and open a follow-up, saying why.
3. **Runtime switching:** change in Settings applies at once with a cross-fade (`tintMs`, off under reduced motion), persists in `DeviceSettings.theme`, per device (per profile later: Inbox, same limits as T-208); the first-boot wizard and the picker use the stored device theme.
4. **Accessibility gate:** a test computes, for each theme, the contrast of `text` and `textDim` against the darkest and the lightest backdrop it can sit on (glass over the lightest hero/wallpaper) and requires >= 4.5:1 (>= 3:1 for large text and the ring against the glass and the backdrop); the high-contrast mode stays above every theme and keeps working with each of them.
5. **Settings UI:** a Theme row (preview chips showing background, glass and tint) in Display & motion or an Appearance pane shared with T-208's wallpaper rows; names and `qsTr`, Turkish.
6. **Lint:** extend `lint-tokens` so a hard-coded colour in QML outside `Theme`/theme sets fails (existing exceptions listed), keeping themes switchable everywhere.

## Out of scope
- User-editable themes or an accent picker (Inbox), per-profile themes, colour matching from a wallpaper or the running game (compare the study's extraction in `04` §3.8), time-of-day automatic switching, theming apps.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Tests: every theme defines every themeable token; switching theme changes `Theme.*` and the screens that read it update in place with focus kept; the non-themeable tokens are identical in all themes; the contrast gate passes for every theme (fails for a deliberately bad fixture theme: the self-check); `DeviceSettings.theme` persists; reduced motion switches without a fade.
- [ ] Screenshots: Home, a Settings pane, the Guide overlay and a modal in each theme, plus the high-contrast mode over one theme; read against `04` §8 (legibility, ring visible).
- [ ] `lint-tokens` fails on a hard-coded colour fixture and passes on the tree.
- [ ] `.\scripts\dev.ps1 test` and `shots` ALL GREEN. Hardware (lead): the owner looks at each theme on the real screen.

## Outcome
Verified: host (hardware look pending, lead run: see "Lead checks")

**Built.** Five built-in colour themes (Dreamglass = today's palette, default; Midnight; Aurora; Ember; Daylight, a real light theme) switchable at run time from Settings -> Display & motion -> **Theme** (the last row; Left/Right cycle, three chips show the ground, glass and tint of the option). The choice is `DeviceSettings.theme` (`display/theme`, per device, invalid values rejected). Every colour token cross-fades over `tintMs` (immediate under reduced motion; a switch in mid-fade starts from the blended look; reduced motion switched on mid-fade finishes it). The picker and the wizard simply read `Theme`, so they use the stored theme.

- **Data.** `shell/qml/themes/`: `ThemeSet.qml` (every themeable token is a `required property`, so a set that forgets one does not compile), `Themes.qml` (the five sets, `tokenNames`, `byKey`), `ThemeMath.qml` (blend, `backdropTop`, blob opacities, WCAG maths shared by the shell and the gate). `Theme.qml` keeps every property name (`readonly property color x: tok("x")`), so **no screen changed** except where a hard-coded colour hid: `Backdrop` (gradient top and blob opacities from `Theme.backdropTop()` / `blobOpacityA/B/C`; objectNames added for the test), `AppArt` (placeholder lightness from `Theme.artTop()` / `artBottom()`, was `Qt.hsla`), and the three `"transparent"` gradient ends of the hero scrim and caption fades (`Theme.heroScrimClear` / `artCaptionFadeClear`, the wash colour at alpha 0, in `Shell.qml` and `ArtCard.qml`).
- **New tokens (all themeable, in every set):** the five tile accents became tokens (`tileAccent()` is still the function), `lightness`, `blobStrength`, `shadowStrength` (the three shadow alphas multiply by it), `artLightTop/Bottom`; the shadow, sheen, rim, `glassTint` and glass-fill tokens of T-204 are themeable too. Not themeable: sizes, type, motion, ring width and offset, `cardSheen` / `glarePeak` / `shimmerPeak` / `glassSheenEnd` (white art effects), `disabledOpacity`, `peekOpacity`, grain (asserted identical across themes by `test_non_themeable_tokens_are_identical_in_every_theme`).
- **Daylight** (all rules met, so five shipped, not four): dark text on frosted white glass over a pale backdrop; white washes instead of night scrims; dark status colours; light danger fill; dark `primaryFill`; shadows at 45 %; `glassTint` white at 55 % (carries dark text); the focus ring is dark navy.
- **Gate** (`tst_theme_contrast.qml`, 04 §7): more than 60 measured pairs per theme (text >= 4.5 on the ground, panel, opaque glass, scrims, hero and caption washes over white and black art, toasts, buttons, and on glass over each Backdrop layer; textDim >= 4.5 on opaque surfaces only; badge and status colours; ring and large text >= 3), computed with `ThemeMath`, the same formulas as `Backdrop`. Daylight also holds its glass text over pure white and pure black (any wallpaper). `test_gate_fails_a_bad_theme` feeds it a Dreamglass with dark text, a ring that sits in the glass and a Daylight with light text: it fails each. **All five pass; the tightest margins** are Aurora's blob overlap 3.04 (floor 3), Dreamglass's 3.63 (floor 3), the badge colours 4.7 to 5.0 (floor 4.5).
- **Lint.** `lint-tokens` now also rejects quoted colour names (not `transparent`) and `Qt.hsla`/`Qt.hsva`, skips `qml/themes/`, and fails if `ThemeSet.qml` and `Themes.tokenNames` disagree; `dev.ps1` has self-checks for both (a fixture with a hard-coded colour of each spelling is rejected; tokens, `transparent` and the theme directory are accepted; a token missing from the list is rejected).
- **Persistence.** `DeviceSettings` has `theme` and `availableThemes` (the C++ list, asserted equal to `Themes.keys`). QML tests cannot restart the singleton, so `tst_main.cpp` runs a C++ check on a temporary ini store (a second instance reads what the first wrote; a stored non-theme falls back) and exposes its result as the context property `themePersistence`, which `test_theme_persists` asserts.

**Tests.** `tst_themes.qml` (21 tests + 5 screenshot rows): five themes and the list agreement; every theme defines every token (type and validity, no two the same); `Theme.*` follows each set (every colour, tiles, blob, shadow and art numbers); Dreamglass equals today's palette; non-themeable tokens identical; reduced motion = no fade; the fade (mid-fade lies between the two looks, no jump when the target changes, reduced motion mid-fade finishes it); an invalid theme is rejected; persistence; the `Backdrop` item follows the theme; the Theme row in a running Shell (focus stays on the row through four switches and a wrap, the label reads the new text colour, the chips show the option, the row follows an external change); the picker uses the stored theme. `tst_theme_contrast.qml` (gate, self-check, pinned limits). `tst_settings.qml`: `test_pane_rows_navigate_and_clamp` now expects the Theme row as the last row (it asserted Screen edge was last; the clamp assertion is kept, on the new last row). **Revert tests** (each change undone, the named test failed, then restored): no finish on reduced motion -> `test_reduced_motion_during_a_fade_finishes_it`; cross-fade starting from the target instead of what is shown -> `test_switch_cross_fades_over_tintMs` and `test_switch_in_mid_fade_starts_from_what_is_shown`; `Backdrop` blob B back to a literal -> `test_backdrop_follows_the_theme`; theme not stored -> `test_theme_persists` (and 13 more); Daylight with light text -> `test_every_theme_passes_the_gate(daylight)`, `test_known_limits` and the Theme row test.

**Screenshots** (`dev.ps1 shots`, `output/shell-shots/review/t209-<theme>-1..5-*.png`: picker, PIN pad (a modal), Home, Guide overlay, Settings with the Theme row; all five themes): read at half size against 04 §8; text is legible and the ring visible in every theme (white on four, dark on Daylight). In addition the **whole existing screenshot suite was run once in Daylight** (a temporary environment switch, not committed) and Home, the Settings panes, Store, Games, the wizard, toasts and OSD, power confirm, PIN, keyboard, the thermal modal and delete-confirm were read: no unreadable text found. Not built: the "high-contrast mode over one theme" shot (the high-contrast theme does not exist yet, `02` §7 plans it; it will be one more set and the gate shows what it must meet).

**Deviations.** (1) The 2 px opposite-lightness ring halo is not built (04 §4: the ring is drawn by 18 separate rectangles; Daylight's dark ring is measured against every surface it sits on instead; Inbox). (2) The gate holds the **theme's default tint**, not every profile avatar colour, and does not promise the dark themes' glass over a *bright wallpaper* (white text on untinted glass over white is 0.9:1) or `glassTint` carrying text on a dark theme; these are pinned in `test_known_limits`, written in 04 §7 and are Inbox items. Dreamglass itself has two real limits recorded there (the blob overlap on bright avatar tints 2.5 to 2.8:1; the avatar initial on the orange, cyan and green avatars 2.2 to 2.9:1). (3) "High-contrast mode stays above every theme": not built yet, so nothing to test. (4) The two night fades used `"transparent"` (transparent black) as their clear end; it is now the wash colour at alpha 0, so Dreamglass can differ by a faint tone at the end of those fades (not asserted by a test).

**Lead checks (hardware).** On the Dell: cycle all five themes in Settings and look at each (Home, a page, the Guide overlay, the PIN pad); watch the 700 ms cross-fade for dropped frames (about 45 token bindings re-evaluate per frame, plus the Backdrop gradient; if it stutters, shorten the fade or snap on the Performance tier); look at Daylight's shadows (45 %) and pale backdrop on the real panel; Midnight on an OLED (true black ground, blobs at 45 %).
