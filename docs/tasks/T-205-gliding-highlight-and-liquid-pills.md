# T-205 — Gliding highlight with velocity stretch, and liquid-glass pills

Status: in-progress · Phase: 2 · Depends: T-204 · Area: shell · Size: M · Owner: builder

## Goal
Moving through the section nav and the Settings categories feels like one highlight gliding between rows (and stretching when you scroll fast) instead of each row switching its fill; on capable GPUs the top-bar pills refract what is behind them. Both from `04` §3.8 (owner, 2026-11-06). Riskier than T-204 (a different focus architecture, a shader), hence the lead.

## Read first
- `docs/specs/04-design-system.md` §3.8 (gliding highlight, liquid pill), §4 focus (the ring never fades during a move; focus is never shown by colour alone), `02-shell.md` (section nav, Settings, accessibility of focus)
- T-204's Outcome (tokens `easeGlide`, `motionScale`, shadows, `GlassLayers`), `GlassPill.qml`, `SectionNav`, `SettingsPage.qml`, `PATTERNS.md` focus rules, the accessibility tests (focus + role assertions)
- B-002/B-003 in `STATUS.md` (real-GPU measurements)

## Scope
1. **Highlight component** (`FocusGlide.qml`): follows the focused item's geometry in a list/row of siblings, glides with `easeGlide` over 280 ms x `motionScale`, retargets from its current position (no jump, no restart from zero) and snaps when `motionScale` is 0. Focus itself stays on the real items (keys, accessibility, hit areas unchanged); the highlight is a visual that never takes focus and never lags behind the item the screen reader announces. Stretch: length factor from velocity (|dx|/dt / 2600 units per s, clamped 0..1, smoothed at 14 per s), off under reduced motion.
2. **Use it** in the section nav (Home/Games/Store/... pills) and the Settings category list; the rows keep their own focus ring/fill as the fallback when the highlight cannot be shown (Performance tier may keep the plain fill). The ring "never fades during a move" rule holds: the highlight is under the ring, the ring is on the item.
3. **Liquid pill (High tier only):** a small `ShaderEffect` per top-bar pill over its blurred crop: edge refraction (bend min(0.5 x h, 30)), a slow moving specular (three sines, 9/15/23 s), computed on one texture per pill; `qsb` shaders built through the shell's CMake (check Qt 6.8 on Fedora and 6.10 here); any failure to compile falls back to the plain pill. Gate by tier and by a measured budget; decide on the Dell whether High keeps it by default.
4. **Docs/spec:** mark the §3.8 rows built or rejected with the measurement; screenshots.

## Out of scope
- Grid card focus (stays per-card), glass over a running app (B-003), refraction on large panels.

## Outcome
Verified: host (the Dell frame times and the next image build are pending, see "Hardware" and "Image")

**Built.**
- `FocusGlide.qml`: the gliding, stretching highlight (04 §3.8). A picture only: `target` (the focused item), `shown` (the owner's `activeFocus`), `orientation`, `u`. It finds the target's geometry by walking the parents' x/y inside a binding (so it follows scrolling, layouts and resizes), exposes `targetX/Y/Width/Height`, animates x/y/width/height with `Behavior`s over `Theme.glideMs` (280 x `motionScale`) with `easeGlide` (a new target restarts the animation from the current value: no jump), and snaps when `motionScale` is 0, on its first placement and while hidden. Stretch: `stretchFor(speed)` (|speed| / 2600, clamped), `stepStretch(dt, speed)` (smoothed at 14 per second, 0 under reduced motion), fed per frame by a `FrameAnimation` that runs only while something moves; the body is longer on the trailing side by `glideStretchMax` (0.5) x stretch x its length. It never takes focus; each item keeps its ring and hides only its own focus *fill* while `glide.active` (the fallback when the highlight is not drawn).
- Used by `SectionNav` (a sibling above the glass, below the pills; a pill that is the current section keeps its white `primaryFill`) and by the Settings category list (`parent: categoryList.contentItem`, so it scrolls with the rows; the list's rows keep `selectedFill` when a pane has focus). The performance tier keeps the highlight too (04 table: all tiers).
- Liquid pill: `shaders/liquid.frag` (GLSL 440; edge refraction with a quadratic falloff over `min(0.5 x height, 30 x u)` px, specular band moved by three sines of 9, 15 and 23 s, antialiased capsule shape; one texture, one pass), built to `liquid.frag.qsb` by `qt_add_shaders` in `shell/CMakeLists.txt` (ShaderTools is an optional component: without it the build succeeds and the pill falls back), drawn by `LiquidGlass.qml` which `GlassPill` creates through a Loader only when `Theme.liquidOn` (High tier and the one switch `Theme.liquidPill`, default `true`). `GlassPill.liquid` is true only when the scene graph is on a GPU and the `.qsb` loaded; otherwise the plain blurred pill draws exactly as before. A missing shader was proven to report `Error` and to fall back (shots test, GPU).
- Tokens added to `Theme.qml`: `glideMs`, `glideSpeedRef`, `glideSmoothing`, `glideStretchMax`, `liquidPill`, `liquidOn`, `liquidBendFactor`, `liquidBendMax`, `liquidPeriods`, `liquidCycleMs`, `liquidBlurFactor`; and the themeable colour `glassSpecular` (all five theme sets, `ThemeSet.qml`, `tokenNames`): colours must follow the theme and none existed for the specular (white 20 to 24 % on the dark themes, white 70 % on Daylight).

**Decisions to know.**
- The liquid pill blurs at 0.4 x `blurMax` (`liquidBlurFactor`): a crop blurred at 64 has no detail left, so the edge refraction bent nothing visible. This changes the High pill's look slightly (a little less frosted) when the liquid look is on.
- `ShaderEffect.status` stayed `Uncompiled` for a working shader on Qt 6.10 / D3D11, so "compiled" means: status is not `Error` and the scene graph API is not Software/Null/Unknown. A GLSL error is caught by `qsb` at build time (the build fails); a driver that rejects a built `.qsb` at pipeline creation is not detectable from QML.
- Test-only: `tst_main.cpp` sets `QML_XHR_ALLOW_FILE_READ` so `tst_glide.qml` can read `liquid.frag.qsb` from the module resource (proves the build step produced it).
- The task asked to "sample the property across a fake clock"; Qt Quick Test cannot fake the animation clock, so `test_retarget_mid_glide_does_not_jump` reads `x` right after the retarget (must equal the mid-glide value within 2 px) and then samples while it settles (every sample stays between where it was and the goal).

**Tests** (`tst_glide.qml`, `tst_glide_settings.qml`): goal geometry equals the focused item for every item (also through offset ancestors, moving/resizing targets, a target outside the parent); glide instead of jump; retarget mid-glide without a jump; reduced motion snaps; first placement and hidden highlight snap; stretch factor, 14/s smoothing, direction and body geometry, none under reduced motion, a real glide stretches and settles; nav: highlight goal = focused pill after every Right, nav keeps focus, the highlight never has `activeFocus`/`focus`, accessible name follows at once, the pill's fill is transparent only while the highlight is drawn (fallback fill when not) and the ring stays, highlight leaves with focus and returns in place, snap under reduced motion; highlight colour follows every theme; Settings list: goal = focused row also after the list scrolled, rows keep ring, fill hidden, pane focus hides it, snaps under reduced motion; liquid: gates by tier and switch, fallback to the plain pill, the `.qsb` is in the module, tokens (periods 9/15/23, cycle 1035 s, bend 30 / 0.5).
Revert checks (each undone, the test named failed, then restored): `Theme.liquidOn && blurMax > 0` -> `Theme.liquidPill` failed `test_liquid_pill_is_gated_by_tier_and_switch`; the pill's `&& !glide.active` removed failed `test_nav_pill_hides_its_own_fill_only_while_the_highlight_is_drawn`; `parent: categoryList.contentItem` removed failed `test_highlight_follows_the_focused_category` (it is what the scrolled-list check caught during development); the reduced-motion guard in `stepStretch` removed failed `test_no_stretch_under_reduced_motion`; `glideMs` without `motionScale` failed `test_glide_tokens`; the `primed` guard removed failed `test_first_placement_snaps`. The extra `Theme.motionScale > 0` guard on the Behaviors alone is redundant (a zero-length animation is already instant), so no test fails without it.

**Screenshots** (`dev.ps1 shots`, real GPU; `output\shell-shots\review\`, read): `glide-nav-mid` (the highlight between Games and Media), `glide-nav-rest`, `glide-nav-stretch` (a three-pill run: the highlight is a longer stroke), `glide-settings-rest`, `glide-settings-mid`, `liquid-pill-high` (+ `-later` after 7 s: the specular moved), `liquid-pill-high-switch-off` (the plain High pill), `liquid-pill-balanced`, `liquid-pill-daylight`. Read against 04 §8: the ring is on the item and shows at once while the fill glides; Daylight's highlight is the dark `focusFill` and its ring dark; the liquid pill's edge bends the stripes of the test stage and the specular band is visible as a soft light patch that moves; no token or component was invented outside the list above. A screenshot cannot catch the stretch at a chosen speed; `glide-nav-stretch` waits for `stretch > 0.08` and shows a longer body. The first screenshot round showed the highlight was invisible in liquid mode (it had `z: -1`, under the opaque shader output); fixed by removing the z.

No image build was run on this machine (the task has no VM criterion): lead, the next image build proves it; if the package name is wrong the build still passes without the shader (optional component) and the pill is the plain one, so the risk is a missing look, not a failed image.
