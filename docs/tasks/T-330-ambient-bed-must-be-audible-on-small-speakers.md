# T-330 — The ambient bed must be audible on a small speaker and on a TV, and the checker must say so

Status: done · Phase: 2 · Depends: T-321 · Area: scripts (make-sounds.py), shell (SoundThemeCheck, fixtures, sound-themes guide), image (sink default volume, as a finding) · Size: M · Owner: lead

## Goal
With T-321 the Dreamglass ambient bed finally plays on the Dell (stream `running` at 24 kHz, no format errors), but the owner hears nothing: the bed has its energy at 73-150 Hz (peak -12 dBFS before gain) and plays at theme gain 0.7, Ambient slider 100 % and a sink volume of 0.40 through the OptiPlex 7080's tiny internal speaker, which does not reproduce below about 200 Hz. A bed that cannot be heard on a 5 cm speaker or a TV is a design failure.

## Scope
1. **Redesign the beds** in `scripts/make-sounds.py` for every bundled and example theme: a soft pad with its energy between about 200 Hz and 4 kHz (fifths and octaves above 200 Hz, gentle low-pass near 4 kHz, little below 120 Hz), seamless loop of whole cycles as today (loop points, `--verify`), a perceived level of about -26 to -22 LUFS integrated before theme gain (quiet but audible on a 5 cm speaker at a normal sink volume) and a true peak below -3 dBFS; the FLAC frame headers keep naming the sample rate (T-321).
2. **Checker rule** (`SoundThemeCheck.cpp`, fixtures, `lint-sound-themes.ps1`, `docs/developers/sound-themes.md`): an ambient file is refused when more than a stated share of its energy is below 150 Hz or when it is nearly silent (RMS window), with a fixture of each (a sub-bass-only bed, a near-silent bed) and an accepted one; keep it cheap and hostile-file-safe (decode with the existing path, bounded work, no hang).
3. **Listen on the Dell** (the owner's standing permission: the Dell is a test machine, root over SSH with `~/.ssh/ruyaos_dev`, `-o StrictHostKeyChecking=no -o UserKnownHostsFile=/dev/null`; overlays in `/run` vanish on reboot; report every change as `Dell: ...`). The runtime overlay of T-321 is still there: `/usr/share/ruyaos/sounds/dreamglass/ambient.flac` is a bind mount of `/run/t321/ambient.flac` and the drop-in `/run/systemd/system/ruya-compositor@tty1.service.d/90-t321-ambient.conf` does it; replace the file in `/run/t321/` with your new bed and restart `ruya-compositor@tty1`. Measure with the sink monitor (`pw-record --target <sink>.monitor`, peak and RMS in the 200-4000 Hz band) and say what the owner should hear; the lead relays "listen now" to the owner. The Dell sink volume was set to 1.00 by the lead for the test.
4. **Finding to record (no code):** the sink volume at first start was 0.40 on the Dell. Say in the Outcome whether that is our default or a leftover (look for `ruya-devices`/WirePlumber defaults) and propose a sane console default (a high sink volume, the TV controls the loudness): that becomes part of T-322.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The regenerated Dreamglass and example beds measure within the stated level and band rules (numbers in the Outcome); loops still whole cycles, `--verify` green.
- [x] The checker refuses the two bad fixtures and accepts the shipped themes; `.\scripts\dev.ps1 test` ALL GREEN.
- [x] On the Dell the new bed is `running` and measured at the sink; the lead gets a sentence to relay to the owner.

**The new beds** (`scripts/make-sounds.py`): a voice is 6 harmonics at h^-1.5 under a gentle low-pass, 1/sqrt(1+(f/4000)^4); Dreamglass is D4, A4, D5, E5 (the fifth above A), A5 and a quiet D3 at 146.8 Hz (level 0.12), each detuned in a pair with slow level drift. Every phase comes from whole numbers (k * i mod n), so the loop is whole cycles. The level is set by loudness, not by peak: `AMBIENT_LUFS = -22.5` (BS.1770 K-weighting and R 128 gating, computed in the frequency domain, which is exact for a periodic loop). Measured by `--verify` (`check_bed`): Dreamglass -22.5 LUFS, true peak -8.6 dBFS (4x band-limited oversampling), 0.0 % below 120 Hz, 0.6 % below 150 Hz, 99.4 % between 200 Hz and 4 kHz, seam step 284 against a biggest inner step of 3067; RMS -22.0 dBFS, A-weighted -25.2 dB. The example theme (A3 220 Hz, E4, A4 at 16 kHz) is -22.5 LUFS, true peak -10.8 dBFS, 100 % between 200 Hz and 4 kHz, seam 535 against 2187. `--verify` now fails when a bed is outside -26 to -22 LUFS, has a true peak of -3 dBFS or more, more than 3 % below 120 Hz or less than 85 % between 200 Hz and 4 kHz (`check_bed` on the T-321 bed gives three of those problems). The FLAC frames still name their rate (rate code 7 for 24 kHz, 5 for 16 kHz; `decode_flac` asserts it) and round-trip. The ambient grew from 618 603 to 1 981 856 bytes (the 3 MB limit holds; more harmonics compress less) and the theme from 1.1 MB to 2.5 MB. `--verify` takes about 14 s.

**Checker** (`SoundThemeCheck.cpp`): a FLAC ambient is now read whole (at most 3 MB) and decoded by the checker's own decoder (RFC 9639: CONSTANT, VERBATIM, FIXED, LPC; Rice with 4- and 5-bit parameters and escapes; wasted bits; independent, left/side, side/right and mid/side). It trusts nothing: every read is bounds-checked, the CRC-8 and CRC-16 of each frame must match, the channel count must match STREAMINFO, partition orders, LPC precision and shift, Rice quotients (at most 2^20) and wasted bits are checked before use, and a decoded sample must fit its bit depth, so every sum stays below 2^53. At most the first minute of the loop is measured and at most 10 minutes of audio are decoded whatever STREAMINFO claims (a loop that starts later passes with a warning). Rules: refused when more than 50 % of the energy is below 150 Hz (two RBJ biquads, a 4th-order Butterworth low-pass at 150 Hz, so a 220 Hz tone counts about 4.5 %) or when the median RMS of the 1 s windows is below -40 dBFS (the file, before the gain), and when a frame is damaged or the audio ends before the loop. Thresholds against the baseline: the T-321 bed reads 69 % below 150 Hz (refused), the new Dreamglass bed 0.5 % and -22.1 dBFS, the example 2.4 % and -21.7 dBFS. `ruya-shell --check-sound-theme` prints both numbers on the `ok` line. An Ogg ambient is not decoded and passes with a warning. Plain words (`SoundThemeText.qml`, en and tr): "The ambient sound is too deep for TV speakers" / "Ortam sesi TV hoparlörleri için fazla pes", "The ambient sound is almost silent" / "Ortam sesi neredeyse sessiz"; a damaged FLAC says "A sound file is not in a format that can be played". `SoundThemes.check` also returns `ambientMeasured`, `ambientLevel` and `ambientLowShare`.

Fixtures: the header-only FLAC fixtures are replaced by real files (`makeFlac`: 16-bit, FIXED order-2 Rice subframes, right CRCs, mono or any stereo layout). New cases: `bad-ambient-sub-bass` (55 + 82.5 Hz: 100 %), `bad-ambient-silent` (440 Hz at 0.004: -51 dBFS), `ok-ambient-some-bass` (100 Hz + 440 Hz: 38 %), `bad-flac-damaged` (a bit flipped in frame 5), `bad-flac-cut-short`, and `ok-flac-left-right`, `-left-side`, `-side-right`, `-mid-side` (left 440 Hz at 0.3, right 100 Hz at 0.1: each decodes to -16.0 dBFS and 9.6 %). The FLAC fuzz seed is a real 5.12 s file, so its first 128 bytes reach the frame header, the subframe header, the warm-up samples and the residual: 4 294 hostile files (3 254 before) in 14.2 s against 10.3 s on main, measured while an image build ran; a fuzz series now writes the unchanged cue and manifest once. Files from a real encoder (ffmpeg on the Dell: LPC, mid/side stereo 16-bit 24 kHz, and mono 24-bit 48 kHz) decode to the levels expected from their source (-23.5 and -21.8 dBFS); the ffmpeg-encoded T-321 bed is refused (69 %). Checking Dreamglass costs about 23 ms (50 checks in 1.19 s).

**Finding for T-322 (no code):** the 0.40 at first start is WirePlumber's own default, neither ours nor a leftover: Fedora's wireplumber 0.5.19 sets `device.routes.default-sink-volume = 0.064` (linear; 0.40 on wpctl's cubic scale, -23.9 dB) for a route it has no saved state for, and nothing in `image/` or `ruya-devices` sets a volume (ruya-devices only calls `wpctl set-volume` when the player moves the slider). The route state is kept in `/var/lib/ruya-shell/.local/state/wireplumber/default-routes` (now 0.027 = 0.30). Proposal: ship a drop-in `/usr/share/wireplumber/wireplumber.conf.d/50-ruyaos-volume.conf` with `wireplumber.settings = { device.routes.default-sink-volume = 1.0 }`, so a console starts at 0 dB and the TV's remote sets the loudness. `apply-routes.lua` reads `device.routes.default-sink-volume` from the device properties first, so if 1.0 is too loud on internal speakers or a headphone jack, a `monitor.alsa.rules` entry can give analog devices a lower one (for example 0.343, 0.70 on the cubic scale).
