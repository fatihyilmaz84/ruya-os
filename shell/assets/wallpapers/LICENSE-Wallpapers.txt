RuyaOS bundled wallpapers (T-208)

Files: dusk.jpg, aurora.jpg, ember.jpg, lagoon.jpg, orchid.jpg, daybreak.jpg, catalog.json

Source: made for this project by scripts/make-wallpapers.py in the RuyaOS repository.
The pictures are computed from gradients, gaussian fields, feathered discs and a seeded
pseudo-random dither. No photograph, stock image, model output or other third-party material
is used or was used as a reference image.
